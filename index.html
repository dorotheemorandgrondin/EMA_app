Instructions
Each day has 8 check-ins: a morning questionnaire, 6 short "beeps" spread through the day, and an evening questionnaire. The row of circles at the top of the home screen shows all 8 — filled green with a check once done, an orange outline when open right now, grey with a dash if missed, and blank if still to come.

Each check-in only opens during its own time window (shown under its name on the home screen). Tap "Start" during that window to answer it. If a window closes before you complete it, it's marked missed and can't be reopened.

Use "Note for the research team" any time to tell us about a difficulty, a question, or anything else — no need to wait for a check-in.
For reliable reminders, install the free ntfy app from the Play Store, open it, tap "+", and paste in the topic code shown in Settings (a short code like dailyrhythm-ab12cd — tap Copy next to it, don't type the words "Reliable notifications"). That's the whole setup — after that, ntfy delivers each reminder on its own, even if this app or Chrome is fully closed.

Tapping a notification opens this app directly into the right questionnaire.
